## How to choose between and among

| Preposition | Used for | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **between** | two clearly separate people or things (or more, seen individually) | The café is **between** the bank **and** the school. |
| **among** | a group or mass — three or more seen together | The house is hidden **among** the trees. |

## When to use *between*

### 1. Two people, things or places

- Sit **between** Laia **and** Pol.
- Barcelona is **between** the sea **and** the mountains.

> ⚠️ Typical error: ~~between Laia or Pol~~ → *Between* goes with **and**: *between Laia and Pol.*

### 2. Time and numbers

- The shop is closed **between** 2 **and** 4 p.m.
- It costs **between** €10 **and** €15.

> ⚠️ Typical error: ~~between 2 to 4~~ → *between 2 and 4* (or *from 2 to 4*).

### 3. Differences, choices and relationships

- What's the difference **between** *say* **and** *tell*?
- I can't choose **between** these two dresses.
- There's a strong friendship **between** them.

### 4. More than two, seen individually

When you name separate items or countries, *between* is correct even with three or more.

- Andorra is **between** France **and** Spain.
- The talks **between** Spain, France **and** Italy went well.

## When to use *among*

### 1. Inside a group or crowd

- I found my keys **among** the papers on my desk.
- She was **among** the best students in the class.

> ⚠️ Typical error: ~~She is between the best students.~~ → Part of a group: *among the best students.*

### 2. Sharing in a group

- The money was shared **among** the five friends.
- *Between* is also common here in informal English.

### 3. Popular *among* a group

- This app is very popular **among** teenagers.

## Pronouns after *between* and *among*

Use **object pronouns**: *me, you, him, her, us, them*.

| ✗ | ✓ |
|---|---|
| ~~between you and I~~ | **between you and me** |
| ~~among we~~ | **among us** |

## *Amongst*

*Amongst* = *among*. It's a bit more formal and old-fashioned, and more common in British English.

## Quick test

| Question | Use |
|---|---|
| Can you name the things, one by one? | **between** |
| Is it a crowd, group or mass? | **among** |

## Common mistakes

**1. *Between … or*.** → **between … and**.

**2. *Between* for a crowd.** *~~between the trees~~* (a forest) → **among the trees.**

**3. *Among* for two.** *~~among the two cities~~* → **between the two cities.**

**4. Subject pronoun.** *~~between you and I~~* → **between you and me.**

**Pro tip:** **between** = individual points (A and B); **among** = inside a crowd.
