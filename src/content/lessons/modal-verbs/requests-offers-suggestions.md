## How to make requests, offers and suggestions

| Function | Modals | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Request (asking someone to do something) | Can / Could / Will / Would **you** …? | **Could you** close the door, please? |
| Offer (saying you'll do something) | Shall / Can **I** …? · I'll … | **Shall I** carry your bag? |
| Suggestion (an idea for both of us) | Shall **we** …? · Let's … · Why don't we …? | **Shall we** go to the beach? |

All these modals take the **base verb**: no *to*, no *-s*, no *-ing*.

## Requests

### 1. Asking someone to do something

The longer and softer the request, the more polite it sounds.

| Less polite → more polite | |
|---|---|
| **Can you** help me? | friends, family |
| **Will you** help me? | direct, a bit stronger |
| **Could you** help me? | polite, everyday |
| **Would you** help me? | polite |
| **Would you mind helping** me? | very polite (+ *-ing*) |

> ⚠️ Typical error: ~~Could you to help me?~~ → No *to* after the modal: *Could you help me?*

### 2. *Would you mind* + -ing

- **Would you mind opening** the window?
- **Would you mind not shouting**?

> ⚠️ Typical error: ~~Would you mind to open the window?~~ → *Mind* takes *-ing*: *Would you mind opening…?*

## Offers

### 1. *Shall I…? / Can I…?*

Use these to ask if the other person wants your help.

- **Shall I** make some tea?
- **Can I** help you with your homework?

### 2. *I'll…* (spontaneous offer)

- That bag looks heavy — **I'll carry** it.
- Don't worry, **I'll pay**.

> ⚠️ Typical error: ~~I carry it for you.~~ (an offer now) → A spontaneous offer uses *I'll*: *I'll carry it for you.*

### 3. *Would you like…?*

- **Would you like** a drink? (+ noun)
- **Would you like to** come with us? (+ *to* + verb)

## Suggestions

### 1. *Shall we…? / Let's…*

- **Shall we** order a pizza?
- **Let's** go to the cinema. (no *to*)

> ⚠️ Typical error: ~~Let's to go to the cinema.~~ → No *to* after *let's*: *Let's go…*

### 2. *Why don't we…? / How about…? / What about…?*

| Phrase | Followed by | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Why don't we | base verb | **Why don't we** take the train? |
| How about / What about | -ing or noun | **How about going** for a walk? |
| We could | base verb | **We could** meet at six. |

## Answering

| Function | Yes | No |
|---|---|---|
| request | Sure. / Of course. / No problem. | Sorry, I can't. |
| offer | Yes, please. / That's very kind. | No, thanks. I'm fine. |
| suggestion | Good idea! / Why not? | I'd rather not. / Maybe another time. |

## Common mistakes

**1. *To* after the modal or *let's*.** *~~Shall we to go?~~* → **Shall we go?**

**2. Wrong form after *how about*.** *~~How about go to the park?~~* → **How about going to the park?**

**3. *Would you mind* + infinitive.** *~~Would you mind to wait?~~* → **Would you mind waiting?**

**4. Answering *Would you mind…?* with *yes*.** *Yes* means you DO mind. To agree, say **No, not at all.**

**Pro tip:** *you* = request (you do it), *I* = offer (I do it), *we* = suggestion (we do it together).
