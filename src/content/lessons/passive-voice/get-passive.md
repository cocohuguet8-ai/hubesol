## How to form the get passive

| Tense | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Present simple | get / gets + past participle | He **gets paid** on Fridays. |
| Past simple | got + past participle | My phone **got stolen**. |
| Present continuous | is / are getting + participle | They**'re getting married**. |
| Present perfect | has / have got(ten) + participle | She**'s got promoted**. |
| Future | will get + participle | You**'ll get hurt**. |

*Get* replaces *be* in the passive. Because *get* is a normal verb, it needs **do / does / did** in questions and negatives.

| ✗ | ✓ |
|---|---|
| ~~Did you got hurt?~~ | **Did** you **get** hurt? |
| ~~He doesn't gets paid.~~ | He **doesn't get** paid. |
| ~~She got arrest.~~ | She **got arrested**. |

## When to use the get passive

### 1. Informal, spoken English

*Get* sounds more natural than *be* in conversation.

- My bike **got stolen** last week. (informal)
- My bike **was stolen** last week. (neutral / formal)

> ⚠️ Typical error: ~~The President got elected in 2020~~ (in a formal report) → In formal writing use *be*: *The President was elected…*

### 2. Something unexpected, unplanned or bad

*Get* often suggests an accident or bad luck.

- Be careful or you'll **get hurt**.
- Our flight **got cancelled**.
- He **got caught** cheating in the exam.

> ⚠️ Typical error: ~~Be careful or you'll get hurting.~~ → *Get* + past participle: *you'll get hurt.*

### 3. Changes and events

- They're **getting married** in June.
- I **got invited** to Laia's party!
- She **got promoted** last month.

## Fixed expressions with *get*

| Expression | Example |
|---|---|
| get married / divorced | They **got married** in Girona. |
| get dressed / changed | I **got dressed** quickly. |
| get lost | We **got lost** in the old town. |
| get hurt / injured | Two players **got injured**. |
| get paid | Do you **get paid** weekly? |
| get fired / sacked | He **got fired** for being late. |
| get stuck | We **got stuck** in traffic. |

## *Get* or *be*?

| | *be* passive | *get* passive |
|---|---|---|
| register | neutral, formal | informal, spoken |
| meaning | state or action | action, change, often unexpected |
| example | The door **was locked**. (state) | The door **got locked**. (it happened) |

*Get* is **not** used with verbs of thinking or with states: ~~She got known as a genius~~ → **She was known as a genius.**

## Questions and negatives

| Question | Negative |
|---|---|
| **Did** you **get** invited? | I **didn't get** invited. |
| **Do** they **get** paid? | They **don't get** paid. |
| **Has** he **got** hurt? | He **hasn't got** hurt. |

## Common mistakes

**1. Base verb instead of participle.** *~~He got arrest~~* → **He got arrested.**

**2. *-ing* instead of participle.** *~~You'll get hurting~~* → **You'll get hurt.**

**3. Wrong auxiliary in questions.** *~~Were you got hurt?~~* → **Did you get hurt?**

**4. *Get* in formal writing.** Use *be* in reports and essays.

**Pro tip:** if you can say "it happened to me" — *I got lost, I got hurt, I got invited* — the get passive fits perfectly.
