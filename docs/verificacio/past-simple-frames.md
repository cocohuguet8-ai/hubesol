## Why no context makes this correct

- Find the mistake 1: Why no context makes this correct: «Last weekend» fixes a finished time, and «go» is a present/base form; no reading makes «I go» correct with a finished time marker in front.
- Find the mistake 2: Why no context makes this correct: A question with «Did» can only be answered with «did/didn’t»; «Yes, I do» answers a present-simple question.
- Find the mistake 3: Why no context makes this correct: «We» takes «were» in the past; «we was» is not standard English in any variety taught here.
- Find the mistake 4: Why no context makes this correct: «Done» is only a participle and needs «have/has/had»; «She done her exercises» is not standard past simple.
- Find the mistake 5: Why no context makes this correct: «Last Sunday» is finished and «Do you play» is present simple; the combination is impossible.
- Find the mistake 6: Why no context makes this correct: Paying £30 «at the shop» is buying; «brought» (from «bring») needs a place she carried it to, and «at the shop for £30» rules that out.
- Find the mistake 7: Why no context makes this correct: days of the week take «on»; «in Monday morning» is not grammatical in any variety.
- Find the mistake 8: Why no context makes this correct: «Yesterday» is finished, so a present auxiliary («doesn’t») cannot go with it.
- Find the mistake 9: Why no context makes this correct: The first and last actions are past («paid», «went»), so the middle verb «leave» cannot stay in the base form in the same list.
- Find the mistake 10: Why no context makes this correct: «Been» is a participle: it needs «have/has/had», and «Last summer we been» has none.
- Find the mistake 11: Why no context makes this correct: Years take «in», not «at»; «at 2021» is not grammatical.
- Find the mistake 12: Why no context makes this correct: «The pizza» is singular, so «Were the pizza good?» is not grammatical.
- Find the mistake 13: Why no context makes this correct: «Didn’t» needs a base verb after it; «late» is an adjective, so «We didn’t late» is impossible.
- Find the mistake 14: Why no context makes this correct: «An hour ago» marks a finished time, and «calls» is present simple; the two cannot go together.
- Find the mistake 15: Why no context makes this correct: «When we were little» sets the past, and «my brother is afraid» is present, so the clause cannot continue in the present.
- Find the mistake 16: Why no context makes this correct: «Opened» and «read» are past in the same list, so «then call» cannot be a base form; it would have to be an imperative, which does not fit.
- Find the mistake 17: Why no context makes this correct: «Did» needs a base verb after the subject; «tired» is an adjective, so «Did you tired» is impossible.
- Find the mistake 18: Why no context makes this correct: «Born» describes the past, and «is born» (present) is not used for a person who was born earlier.
- Find the mistake 19: Why no context makes this correct: «I» takes «was» in the past; «I were» only appears in conditional «if I were…» sentences, and this is not one.
- Find the mistake 20: Why no context makes this correct: «Born» in a main clause needs «was/were» before it; without it the sentence is a fragment, even with «but he lives in Girona now».
- Level check 17: Why no context makes this correct: «The shops» is plural, so «was» cannot agree with it in the past.
- Level check 18: Why no context makes this correct: «Gone» is a participle and needs «have/has/had»; «The children gone to bed at nine» is not standard past simple.
- Level check 19: Why no context makes this correct: «Got» puts the story in the past, so «so we miss the start» cannot be a present verb in the same sentence.
- Level check 20: Why no context makes this correct: «Two weeks ago» marks a finished time, and «are» is present; the two cannot go together.
